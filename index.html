1.2 Profile

Paulo Benício Melo Sousa is a Brazilian IT Project Manager / Business Analyst and academic lecturer now based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. His career splits cleanly into two threads that almost always ran in parallel: leading technology teams inside Brazil's public sector (culminating in managing government-wide systems for the State of Ceará's Finance Secretariat) and teaching computer science at the university level most evenings for nearly two decades. Since 2019 he has taught full-time at Algonquin College in Ottawa, and since 2025 has been deep in a self-directed return to his doctoral subject — quantum computing — stacking up certifications from IBM, Google, and the Linux Foundation.
